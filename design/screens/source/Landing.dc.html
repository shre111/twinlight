Fair questions

How is this different from a survey?
A survey tells you whether people like the idea. Twinlight adds where they live, who they talk to, and which store has to reach them in ten minutes, then shows what that does to your orders and your stock.
Where do the people come from?
They are synthetic. We start from census-aligned synthetic personas and population maps, place them in real buildings from OpenStreetMap, and connect them as households, neighbours, friends and colleagues. No real customer data goes in.
How wrong can it be?
Every number is a range across 50 rehearsals. Give us a past launch and we rehearse it blind, then show how often your real sales landed inside our ranges. Until you do, every page says the rehearsal isn't checked against real sales yet.
